## Third-party material in the Kryptos K4 program

The K4 ciphertext and the published crib letters are part of Jim Sanborn's sculpture *Kryptos* (1990). They are quoted for research and commentary, with sources cited in each note, and are **not** licensed by this repository. Statements by Sanborn, Ed Scheidt and journalists are summarized and linked rather than reproduced. No photograph of the sculpture is included.

The 3D model page shows only the 97 letters of K4 and the seven Morse phrases of the entrance (K0, as transcribed on Wikipedia); every other carved letter is replaced by a generic slot. Its dimensions, bearings and layout were estimated from public photographs (Jim Gillogly; Carol M. Highsmith, Library of Congress, public domain), Esri World Imagery, Elonka Dunin's pages, Monet Friedrich's 2007 diagrams and rumkin.com. These sources are cited, not reproduced.

This conservative initial state avoids accidentally granting rights the maintainer may not hold. A future release may add separate licenses for text, software, and datasets after provenance review. Until then, you may still report replications, bugs, and critiques by linking to the relevant public material.
