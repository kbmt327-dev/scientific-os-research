---
title: Publication methodology
description: How an internal Episode becomes a public Research Note.
---

Publication is a fail-closed projection, not automatic copying.

1. Select an internal Episode and freeze its identifier and SHA-256 digest.
2. Build a public note without copying private paths or unrelated internal context.
3. Separate pre-result predictions from post-result interpretation.
4. State what the evidence supports and what it does not support.
5. Expose the shortest honest reproduction path, including `not yet public` when necessary.
6. Run schema, section, link, secret, and private-path checks.
7. Record editorial, scientific, domain-expert, and peer review separately. Editorial review does not imply scientific validation.
8. Build the actual site and inspect desktop and mobile rendering.
9. Publish only after the public repository passes the checks.

The current Publication Compiler creates a blank projection skeleton and validates completed notes. It deliberately does not summarize claims or decide whether a note is scientifically publishable; those steps still require human judgment.
