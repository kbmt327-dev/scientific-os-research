---
title: What is Open Research Lab?
description: A public projection of private research state, designed for external audit.
---

Open Research Lab publishes selected research produced through Scientific OS with enough context for a third party to inspect the claim, locate the evidence, attempt reproduction, and state a counterexample.

It is not a public mirror of a private workspace. Private research may contain machine paths, personal material, credentials, unfinished reasoning, or unrelated records. Publication therefore creates a reviewed projection with a narrower claim and an explicit evidence boundary.

```text
Internal Episode != Public Research Note
```

The Episode preserves research history for the system. The Research Note is a stable, privacy-reviewed view for external readers. Corrections append public evidence or revise the note with history; they do not rewrite sealed predictions.

## What earns trust here

Trust is not based on the authority of an AI system. It is earned through inspectable artifacts, reproducible procedures, falsifiable claims, visible failures, explicit uncertainty, and independent replication.
