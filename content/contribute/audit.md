---
title: Audit and challenge
description: Report defects, counterexamples, and failed replications.
---

Use an Issue for a concrete defect in code, data, metadata, links, or privacy. Use Discussions for a replication report, competing interpretation, falsification proposal, or broader critique.

Useful reports identify the Research Note ID and commit, distinguish observation from inference, preserve failed results, and propose the smallest discriminating check. A critique does not need to contain a fix.
