## How this lab works

Every Research Note separates the finding from its reach. It shows what changed, what failed, what remains `UNKNOWN`, and the shortest honest reproduction path. Start with **[[en/how-to-read/index|How to read a Research Note]]**; inspect the [[en/about/scientific-os|Scientific OS loop]] only if you need the system behind it.

## Contribute evidence

The valuable next event is not a star. It is an independent rerun, a failed reproduction, a counterexample, or a better observation. **[[en/contribute/index|Choose how to reproduce, challenge, extend, or collaborate →]]**
