In a badminton smash, available preparation time and the body's centre of mass often change together, making their effects difficult to separate from observation alone. This program designs an experiment that manipulates the two factors independently.

## The question

How do short preparation time and a backward centre of mass each affect smash movement and outcome? A 2×2 assignment is designed to separate them.

## Current public boundary

Only the protocol and power sensitivity are public. There are no observations, the design has not been sealed, and data collection is not authorized. This is not a result-bearing study yet.
