---
title: Falsification
description: How public notes state observations that would weaken their claims.
---

Every Finding identifies concrete observations that would weaken or reject its current claim. A Method states negative controls that must be rejected. A Protocol states manipulation or execution failures that prevent causal interpretation.

A surprising mismatch first triggers checks of the instrument, analysis path, and measurement contract. This does not immunize the hypothesis: corrections and alternative explanations must be recorded, and unresolved mismatches remain visible.
