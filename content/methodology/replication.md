---
title: Replication
description: How external replication status is recorded.
---

An independent replication is work performed outside the original research process using a declared version of the note and artifacts. A rerun by the same process is a reproducibility check, not independent replication.

Each note starts with independent replications, failed replications, confirmed bugs, and open critiques at zero. Reports should include the tested commit, environment, command, inputs, observed output, and deviations from the published procedure.
