# Open Research Lab v2 — design decisions

This record keeps the few editorial and interface decisions that materially shape the public research surface.

## Baseline and delta

- Baseline: commit `228782a`, after publication of GPU scheduling EP-0004.
- Preserve: dated claims, later corrections, sealed predictions, evidence hashes, reproduction commands, artifact paths, and existing public URLs.
- Change: the reading order, bilingual symmetry, public navigation, note openings, and machine-reader entry point.
- Do not inherit: lined-paper decoration, English-as-unmarked-default semantics, or publication-operator instructions as the public methodology.

## Selected thesis

The site is a **claim-to-evidence descent**. A reader first sees the current claim and its boundary, then the causal or temporal relation, and only then the complete audit record and reproduction path.

The nearest viable alternative was a research catalog of equal cards. It was rejected because EP-0001 through EP-0004 are not equal peers: later studies narrow earlier claims, and flattening them would hide the update.

The accepted trade-off is a denser flagship sequence on the language home pages. It costs some visual simplicity, but makes correction history visible before a visitor reads an obsolete claim as current.

## Consequential form choices

| Decision | Source relation | Intended reading | Test |
|---|---|---|---|
| Four-stage depth rail | Discover → Understand → Inspect → Reproduce | Deeper evidence is available without front-loading it | 5-second, 30-second, 5-minute reads |
| GPU program shown as a four-step public revision | EP-0004 demotes EP-0003's practical reach; EP-0002/3 narrow EP-0001 | Published research changes under evidence without implying every private update is public | Links and chronology |
| `Shows` and `Does not show` paired, never collapsed | Every claim has an evidence boundary | Scope is part of the finding | Required-section validation |
| UNKNOWN rendered as an open frontier | UNKNOWN is a legitimate research result | Missing evidence invites a next observation | Keyboard and responsive render |
| Quartz graph/explorer remain secondary | Navigation is not the research object | Research content leads; garden tools assist | Desktop/mobile render |
| Japanese and English are parallel views | Neither language is the source/master view | Same research identity, equal access | Pair validation and `hreflang` |

## Revision condition

Revisit the structure when an external replication, critique, or a second multi-note research program exists. Those events may justify a contribution activity view or a program-level comparison that is not warranted yet.
